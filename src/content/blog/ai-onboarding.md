The first few weeks after a client signs with an accounting firm set the tone for the entire relationship. Unfortunately, that's often also the most manual, most email-heavy part of the whole engagement — document requests, engagement letters, intake forms, scheduling, all chased down one message at a time.

An automated onboarding flow doesn't remove the human relationship from that process. It removes the busywork around it.

## What gets automated

**Engagement letters and e-signatures.** Instead of a back-and-forth over email, a new client receives their engagement letter automatically the moment they're marked as won, with e-signature built in and a reminder if it hasn't been signed within a few days.

**Document collection.** Rather than one long email listing everything needed, clients get a structured, trackable request — with automatic reminders for whatever's still outstanding, so nobody on the team has to manually chase a missing W-2.

**Scheduling.** The client books their kickoff call directly into an available slot, with automatic confirmations and reminders, instead of five emails trying to find a time that works.

**Internal handoff.** The team member taking over the relationship gets notified automatically with everything they need — no separate "here's a new client" email required.

## Where AI comes in

Beyond simple automation, AI workflows can handle the parts that used to require judgment: reading an uploaded document and flagging what's missing, drafting a personalized welcome message based on the client's situation, or triaging an incoming question to the right person before a human ever sees it.

## Why it matters more than it seems

None of this is glamorous work, which is exactly why it's worth automating. The time a team saves on onboarding logistics is time that goes back into actual client work — and the client's first experience of the firm becomes "this was easy," instead of "this took three weeks of email tag."
